01 — THE TEAM

Built by engineers.
Driven by flight.

AeroBMSCE is the official aerospace, aeromodelling and unmanned systems innovation club of BMS College of Engineering, operating from Propel Lab–3.

Established in 2012, the team brings together engineers from more than 10 departments to design, build and test advanced aircraft, drones and autonomous systems.
